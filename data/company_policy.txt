COMPANY QUOTATION POLICY

The company provides laptop quotations based on customer requirements.

The system should search available supplier products before preparing a quotation.

The recommended product should match the requested processor, RAM and storage.

The quotation should stay within the customer's maximum budget whenever possible.

Products with longer warranty should be preferred when prices are similar.

The final quotation should contain complete pricing details and supplier information.

All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).
