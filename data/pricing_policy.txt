PRICING POLICY

The default profit margin for quotations is 10 percent.

GST is calculated at 18 percent.

Quotation Calculation:

Base Amount = Supplier Price Per Unit × Quantity

Profit Amount = Base Amount × Profit Margin / 100

Amount After Profit = Base Amount + Profit Amount

GST Amount = Amount After Profit × GST Rate / 100

Final Quotation Amount = Amount After Profit + GST Amount

The quotation should clearly show:

Product Name
Supplier
Quantity
Price Per Unit
Base Amount
Profit Margin
GST
Final Amount
